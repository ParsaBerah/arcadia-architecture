OUR STORY

Building with
purpose since 2010

We believe that good architecture is not just about the buildings, but about the people who inhabit them.

Founded in 2010 by Parsa Berahman, Arcadia started as a small boutique studio with a grand vision: to redefine modern living through sustainable and deeply human-centric design.

Over the past decade, our team has grown into a diverse group of architects, interior designers, and urban planners. We approach every project as a unique challenge, carefully studying the environment, culture, and specific needs of our clients.

Our philosophy is rooted in timelessness. We don't follow fleeting trends; instead, we aim to create spaces that will stand the test of time and age beautifully alongside the people who use them.
